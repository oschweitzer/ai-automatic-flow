# StatusPill

Status pills say who or what a ticket waits on, always with words.

`you` (amber) is anything waiting on the developer: plan to approve, escalated MR, report to read. `client` (sky) is parked_needs_info. `ok` and `bad` are CI and end states. `run` names the AI model or a stage. `mute` shows a raw Jira status. Write who first, then what: "toi · plan à valider". Never a pill with a colour and no word.
